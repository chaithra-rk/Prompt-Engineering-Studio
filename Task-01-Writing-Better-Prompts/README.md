# Task 01 – Writing Better Prompts

## Objective

Understand how prompt clarity, context, audience, tone, and output format affect AI-generated responses.

---

## Prompt 1 – Basic Prompt

### Input

Tell me about climate change.

### Output

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns. It is mainly caused by human activities such as burning fossil fuels and deforestation.

### Observation

The response is general because the prompt does not specify the audience, level of detail, format, or specific information required.

---

## Prompt 2 – Improved Prompt

### Input

Explain climate change to a high-school student in simple language. Include its main causes, effects, and two practical ways students can help reduce its impact. Use short paragraphs and bullet points.

### Output

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns.

### Main Causes

- Burning fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and gas
- Deforestation
- Industrial activities
- Transportation

### Effects

- Rising global temperatures
- Melting glaciers
- Rising sea levels
- Extreme weather events

### What Students Can Do

1. Reduce unnecessary electricity usage.
2. Use public transport, walking, or cycling when possible.

---

## Comparison

| Aspect | Basic Prompt | Improved Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Low | High |
| Audience | Not specified | High-school students |
| Detail | General | Specific |
| Format | Not specified | Bullet points |
| Relevance | General | Targeted |

## Conclusion

A clear and specific prompt produces a more focused and useful response.
