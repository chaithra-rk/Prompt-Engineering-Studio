# Task 02 – Prompting for Creativity

## Objective

Explore how prompt structure, tone, and examples influence creative AI outputs.

---

## Prompt

Generate 3 creative startup ideas for college students.

For each startup include:

1. Startup name
2. Problem
3. Target users
4. Main features
5. Revenue model

Keep the ideas practical, creative, and suitable for college students.

---

# Output 1

## StudySync

**Problem:** Students struggle to organize group study sessions.

**Target Users:** College students

**Features:**
- Create study groups
- Schedule study sessions
- Share notes
- Track study progress

**Revenue Model:** Premium subscriptions and college partnerships.

---

# Output 2

## SkillSwap

**Problem:** Students want to learn new skills but cannot always afford courses.

**Target Users:** College students

**Features:**
- Student-to-student skill exchange
- Skill profiles
- Online sessions
- Ratings and reviews

**Revenue Model:** Premium memberships.

---

# Output 3

## CampusCart

**Problem:** Students need daily-use products quickly on campus.

**Target Users:** College students

**Features:**
- Stationery delivery
- Snacks and essentials
- Book delivery
- Campus-based delivery

**Revenue Model:** Delivery fees and seller commissions.

---

## Comparison

| Idea | Creativity | Practicality | Target Users |
|---|---|---|---|
| StudySync | High | High | Students |
| SkillSwap | High | High | Students |
| CampusCart | High | High | Students |

## Conclusion

The prompt generated multiple creative ideas while maintaining a consistent structure. Providing clear requirements helped produce detailed and organized outputs.
